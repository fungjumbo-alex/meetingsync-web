Privacy Policy

Effective: October 29, 2026

MeetingSync ("we", "the app") is a meeting recording and transcription app for iPhone and Apple Watch, developed by Alex Fung. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. We have no advertising network and no third-party analytics, and the app does not require an account to use.

1. What the app processes

Audio and transcripts. Recording, speech recognition and transcription take place on your device. Transcripts, recordings and your app history are stored locally on your iPhone.

Photos. If you add a photo (for example a photo of a whiteboard), the image is stored locally and attached to the related transcript.

2. Online Recognition (optional)

MeetingSync has an Online Recognition setting (on by default). When it is enabled and a network connection is available, audio is sent to Apple for speech recognition through Apple's built-in speech service, subject to Apple's privacy practices. When the setting is off, or when no network is available, all recognition happens on-device and no audio leaves your iPhone.

You can turn Online Recognition off any time in the app's Settings. Apple's privacy policy may be found at apple.com/legal/privacy.

3. Local storage & deletion

Your recordings, transcripts and photos stay on your device unless you export or share them. You can delete individual recordings at any time from the app. If you delete and uninstall the app, associated local data is removed with it.

4. Export & sharing

Exporting (e.g. as text or PDF) or sharing a transcript is initiated by you, and shares only the content you choose to apps you select on your device.

5. Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect, store or transmit your recordings or transcripts to our own servers.
  • We do not use tracking, analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us by email: alex.sc.fung@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
