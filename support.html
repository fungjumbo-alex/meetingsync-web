Common questions

Where does transcription happen? Recording and transcription run on your iPhone. When you turn on Online Recognition, audio is sent to Apple's servers for dictation; turn it off to process entirely on-device.

Does it work with my Apple Watch? Yes — record audio from your Watch and MeetingSync on your iPhone transcribes it.

Is my data private? Transcripts and recordings are stored locally on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy.
